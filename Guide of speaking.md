#### 2. Introduction & The Slogan

"The movie is about Walter Mitty, a quiet and introverted man. He **has worked** at _Life_ Magazine **for** sixteen years. He spends a **bunch** of time imagining he is a brave hero, but in reality, he just freezes up. He likes a coworker named Cheryl, but he feels unworthy.

Everything changes when he is assigned a mission to find the missing photo negative #25 for the last print issue. This film revolves around _Life_ magazine's slogan: _'To see the world, things dangerous to come to, to see behind walls, draw closer, to find each other, and to feel. That is the purpose of life.'_

Initially, this motto meant nothing to him, but **since** he started his journey, it **has become** his way of living."

#### 3. Main Body: Walter's Adventures (Present Perfect vs. Simple Past)

"This film mixes comedy, action, and drama. It is a thought-provoking movie because it makes you ask: _'What have I been doing with my life all this time? Am I where I truly want to be?'_

During his adventure, Walter does things he never imagined:

- He **has fought** against a shark in the freezing ocean. (In Greenland, he **jumped** out of a helicopter into the water!)
    
- He **has escaped** a dangerous volcano eruption. (When he **was** in Iceland, he **rode** a skateboard to save his life).
    
- He **has climbed** the Himalayas, where he **met** the photographer Sean O'Connell and **saw** a rare snow leopard."
    

#### 4. Travel Tips Inspired by the Movie

"Since this is a movie about stepping out of your comfort zone, I want to share a **bunch** of **travel tips** based on Walter's experiences:

1. **Be prepared for frightening situations:** Stepping into the unknown can be **frightening**, like traveling alone or jumping into new adventures. However, taking risks leads to personal growth.
    
2. **Try local food without fear:** When you travel, you will encounter unusual food. Sometimes it might look or smell a bit **disgusting** at first, but trying local dishes is part of the cultural experience!
    
3. **Connect with locals:** Walter received help from local people in Greenland and Iceland. Being open and friendly with locals makes any trip safer and richer.
    
4. **Live in the moment:** In the movie, the photographer decides not to take a photo of the snow leopard just to enjoy the moment. My main travel tip is: put your phone down sometimes and just enjoy what you are seeing!"
