# Introduction
The movie is about Walter Mitty. Walter is a normal person with a Job at Life Magazine, he wastes time imagining he's a hero, but he just freezes up. He likes a coworker, but be feels unworthy. All that will change when he is assigned a mission to find the perfect photo for the last print Issue. 

This film revolves around Life magazine slogan: "Look at the world, discover dangerous things, look beyond the walls, get closer, meet people and feel. That is the purpose of life." Initially, this means nothing to the main character, but as time passes it begins to make sense to him.
